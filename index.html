Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

The controller of this "Velori" (Wedding) application under the GDPR is Lukas Varga, Herzogenaurach, Germany. This Privacy Policy applies to users worldwide — including the EU/EEA (GDPR), the United Kingdom, the United States including California (CCPA/CPRA), and other countries. For any questions about data protection, you can contact us at any time at lukasvargaaa@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Within the app, we process the following personal data: your email address and password in encrypted form (registration and login); partner names, the wedding date and planning data (tasks, budget, expenses, day timeline, calendar); guest data entered by the couple (guest names, attendance status, seating plan); photos you upload (couple profile, vendor photos); vendor profile data (name, category, city, contact details, prices, description); messages between couples and vendors and vendor reviews; content reports and your list of blocked users; purchase and Premium subscription status data (no payment details); and technical data needed to deliver push notifications (device push token).

3. Why we collect this data

We process data to provide the app's features (wedding planning, sharing the plan with your partner, communication with vendors, account and subscription management) on the basis of contract performance under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We process reports, blocking and content moderation on the basis of our legitimate interest in user safety and abuse prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We send push notifications only with your consent (permission on your phone), which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings. Guest data is entered by the couple, who is responsible for being allowed to store it in the app.

4. Processors, recipients and storage location

We use the following processors (Art. 28 GDPR): Supabase Inc. – database, authentication and file storage; data is stored in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland, EU). RevenueCat Inc. – Premium subscription management (user identifier and subscription status, no payment details). Google (Google Play) – subscription payments; neither the app nor its operator can see your card number. Expo / 650 Industries, Inc. – push notification delivery. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – technical delivery of push notifications to Android devices. Resend Inc. – sending emails from the app (sign-up confirmation, password reset); processes the email address; sent from the EU (Ireland), possible transfer to the USA on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses. RevenueCat, Expo and Google may also process data in the USA; such transfers are protected by EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

5. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Velori is intended for users aged 16 and older (see our Terms of Service) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in accordance with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information may contact us at lukasvargaaa@gmail.com to request its deletion.

6. Your rights if you live in California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, the right to request its deletion, the right to correct inaccurate information, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To exercise these rights, contact us at lukasvargaaa@gmail.com.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR (for users in the EU/EEA and UK) you have the right to access your personal data, rectify it, restrict its processing, request data portability, and in particular the right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR). You can permanently delete your account and all related data at any time directly in the app via Menu → Delete account, or request deletion by emailing us — we offer this same deletion right to all users regardless of country.

8. Retention period

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When your account is deleted (in the app or on request by email, within 30 days at the latest), we permanently remove your data, including photos, messages and wedding data; if you share the wedding with a partner, the wedding data stays with your partner and only your membership is removed. We keep content reports for up to 12 months after they are resolved to prevent abuse; the link to your account is removed when the account is deleted. Payment records are kept by Google Play under its own legal obligations.

9. Contact and right to complain

If you have any questions about the processing of your data (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or COPPA requests), please contact us at lukasvargaaa@gmail.com. Users in the EU/EEA have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in their country; California residents may contact the California Privacy Protection Agency.
