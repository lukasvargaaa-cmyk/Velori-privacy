1. What We Collect

We collect the following data when you use Velori:

  • Email address and password (for authentication)
  • Names of the couple and wedding date
  • Guest list and seating arrangements
  • Budget and expense data
  • Messages between couples and vendors
  • Profile photos (optional)

2. Why We Collect It

Your data is processed to provide the wedding planning service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — contract performance).

3. Data Processor

We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our data processor for secure storage and authentication. Supabase complies with GDPR requirements.

4. Data Retention

Your data is stored until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app under Menu → Delete Account.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (right to erasure)
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
